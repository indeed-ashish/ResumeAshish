Project 02 • Educational Pedagogy & CUET Academic Initiative

The NerdsHub / The Helping Nerd

An academic empowerment initiative focused on creating accessible educational resources to support students preparing for the Common University Entrance Test (CUET) and senior secondary commerce examinations.

While rigorously managing my own preparation for the CUET (where I achieved an 828/1000 score), I conceptualized, wrote and recorded detailed pedagogical breakdowns of core commerce topics, examination strategies, and syllabus guidelines to support peers with free, high-clarity revision material.

  • Educational Content Creation: Structured chapter breakdowns in Business Studies, Economics and Accountancy.
  • Teaching & Explanation: Translating intricate concepts into intuitive, easy-to-digest visual tutorials.
  • Academic Resource Development: Creating curated frameworks, notes, and revision checklists.
  • Time Management: Balancing demanding personal entrance exam preparation with altruistic content creation.
  • Student Engagement: Direct Q&A support, mentorship responses and motivational academic guidance.
